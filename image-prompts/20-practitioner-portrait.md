# 20 — Practitioner portrait

**`practitioner-rasanjana.webp` · 3:4 portrait · 1086 × 1448 · arch crop**

> **This one is not a generation prompt. It has to be a real photograph of
> Dr. Rasanjana Ilangkoon.**
>
> Every other file in this folder is a prompt because the subject is a bottle, a
> leaf or a pair of anonymous hands. This subject is a named, real person whose
> qualification the page states. A synthesised face standing in for that person
> would misrepresent them to every visitor who reads the name beside it, and it
> would undercut the one thing the section exists to establish. Take a photograph.
>
> Until the real photograph exists, the placeholder in place is a flat sage panel
> at the correct dimensions, so the layout is already final.

## Brief for the photographer

The site is built on soft, warm, natural light and a narrow palette. Match it and
the portrait will sit in the page without any retouching.

**Light.** Daylight from a window, falling from one side, diffused — a thin
curtain or an overcast day is ideal. No flash, no ring light, no hard shadow
across the face. Low contrast throughout.

**Background.** A plain warm cream or off-white wall, or the dispensary shelving
well out of focus. Nothing busy behind the head. Avoid anything blue, grey or
cool-toned; the whole site is warm cream, forest green and reddish-brown wood.

**Framing.** Vertical, 3:4. Head and upper body, roughly waist up. Leave calm
space above the head and on both sides — **the corners of this image are cropped
away into a rounded arch**, so nothing important may sit near an edge. Eyes
roughly a third of the way down the frame.

**Expression and posture.** Facing the camera, settled and unhurried. Not a
corporate headshot smile and not a stern clinical stare. The page's claim is that
a real person reads what you send, so the portrait should look like someone you
could write to.

**Wardrobe.** Plain, matte, undyed or muted. Cream, off-white, natural linen or a
soft mid-green all work. Avoid patterns, logos, bright white, and anything with
visible branding or lettering.

**Setting props (optional).** If shooting in the dispensary, a shelf of amber
jars or drying herbs behind, thrown well out of focus, ties the portrait to the
rest of the photography. Keep any labels out of frame or turned away.

**Technical.** Shoot at the highest resolution available; 1086 × 1448 is the
delivered size, not the capture size. Deliver an unretouched original as well as
any edit, so the crop can be adjusted to the arch without re-shooting.

## Optional second frame

If the shoot allows it, a working shot is worth having for later use: hands at
the bench grinding or filling, taken in the same light, with the face out of
frame or out of focus. The site already leans on hands-at-work imagery and one
more would be useful.
